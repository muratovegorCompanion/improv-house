Privacy Policy

Improv House, Koh Phangan, Thailand · last updated 14 October 2026

Improv House rents out its hall by the hour. Bookings are made on WhatsApp or on our Facebook Page in Messenger, where an automated assistant answers questions, finds free time, takes bookings and passes payment confirmations to the owners. This page explains what that assistant keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted.

Improv House is run by its owner, Iegor Muratov, who is responsible for this data (the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act).

What we keep

  • Your phone number and WhatsApp name (on WhatsApp) or your Messenger ID (on Facebook Messenger, an ID that Meta gives for our Page), and the name you give us.
  • Our conversation: the messages you send the assistant and its replies.
  • Your bookings: date and time, price, amount paid, whether it is a party, and its status (held, confirmed, cancelled); and, if you asked to wait for a taken time, your place in that queue.
  • Pictures you send, such as a bank transfer screenshot or a photo of cash left in the hall, together with the payment details read from them.
  • Your language, so we reply in it, and the time of your last message.

We do not ask for or keep payment card numbers, passwords or identity documents.

Why

Only to answer you, to make, change and cancel your bookings, to check payments, and to let the owners step in when the assistant cannot help. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who else sees it

  • The owners of Improv House, through a private Telegram chat: questions the assistant passes on, payment pictures to approve, and booking notices, with your number and name.
  • Telegram carries those owner notices.
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger) carries the messages between you and us.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model (Claude) that reads your messages and payment pictures and writes the replies.
  • Google Calendar holds the hall's schedule; a booking appears there with your name.
  • Supabase stores the data above on servers in Singapore.

Each of them processes the data only to provide that service to us.

How long

We keep your data while it is needed for your bookings and our records. Nothing is deleted automatically yet; anything you ask us to delete is deleted as described below.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it, and you can complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee. Ask on WhatsApp at the number below or in Messenger on our Facebook Page; we answer within 30 days.

Deleting your data

Send the message “Delete my data” on WhatsApp to +66 98 308 3750, from the number you used with us, or in Messenger to the Improv House Facebook Page from the account you used. The owners will delete your conversation, pictures, name and bookings from our systems and remove your name from the hall calendar within 30 days, and will confirm in the same chat once it is done.

A booking that is already paid for and still ahead can be cancelled first if you wish; the usual cancellation terms apply to any refund.

Questions

Write to us on WhatsApp at +66 98 308 3750.
